FAQ

Questions Homeowners Ask

What does a complimentary project review involve?

We review your plans, talk through your goals and give you honest feedback on feasibility, a realistic budget and next steps. There's no obligation. You'll come away knowing whether your project is ready to go ahead.

Do I need plans before contacting you?

No. We can guide you through the whole process with our own architects, designers and engineers.

Do you work with my architect or designer?

Yes, often. We can work within an existing design brief. If you've already engaged an architect, we come on as your builder and manage construction.

Can you help if I'm still refining my design or selections?

Yes. We help with selections and decisions so the finished home fits your vision and your budget. Making the key decisions early is what keeps cost and timeline on track.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Limitless Paving Pty Ltd ("Limitless Paving", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you enquire about or use our concreting services, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can access or correct it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. The information we collect

Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details, such as your name, phone number, email address and postcode or suburb.
  • Project details, such as the type of work you're after (driveway, paths, steps and so on), preferred finish, timeframe, your property address, and photos or measurements of the area.
  • Quote and job details, such as quotes, invoices, payment records and notes from site visits and calls.
  • Communications, including records of calls, SMS, emails and messages with us.
  • Website and advertising data, such as the pages you visit, the ad you clicked, your browser and device type, IP address, and cookie and click identifiers (see section 4).

We don't ask for sensitive information (such as health or financial account details) to provide our services. You can enquire without giving your full name, but we'll need a way to contact you to arrange a site visit or quote.

2. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you:

  • fill in a form on our website or landing pages;
  • submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram;
  • call, text, email or message us;
  • book or attend a site visit or design consult; or
  • accept a quote and engage us to do work.

We also collect some information automatically through cookies and similar technologies when you visit our web pages, and we may receive information from our advertising partners (such as Meta) about how you came to our site.

3. Why we collect and use it

We use your personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and call you to discuss your project;
  • book and carry out site visits and design consults;
  • prepare quotes, schedule and complete work, and invoice you;
  • follow up on quotes and provide after-sales support;
  • send you appointment reminders and project updates;
  • send you occasional marketing messages (you can opt out at any time, see section 7);
  • measure and improve our advertising, including showing relevant ads and measuring which ads lead to enquiries and jobs; and
  • meet our legal, tax and insurance obligations.

4. Cookies, the Meta Pixel and advertising

Our web pages use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API. These tools tell Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram) when someone visits our page or submits an enquiry, so we can measure how our ads perform and show our ads to people who are more likely to be interested.

When you submit a form, we may share your email address, phone number, name, postcode and country with Meta in a hashed (scrambled) form, together with cookie and click identifiers. Meta uses this to match the enquiry to an ad, not to read your details. Meta handles this information under its own privacy policy and terms.

We also record which ad or campaign you came from (for example, UTM tags in the web address) so we know which marketing is working.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage how Meta uses your data for ads in your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences. Blocking cookies won't stop you enquiring with us.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only where needed for the purposes above, with:

  • our marketing and lead management agency, which manages our advertising, receives enquiries and calls new enquiries on our behalf to arrange site visits;
  • software providers we use to run the business, such as our customer relationship management (CRM) system, scheduling, phone and SMS, email, website hosting, accounting and payment tools;
  • advertising platforms, such as Meta, as described in section 4;
  • subcontractors and suppliers where they need details to deliver your job (for example, a site address for a concrete delivery);
  • our professional advisers, such as accountants, insurers and lawyers; and
  • anyone else where you've agreed, or where we're required or authorised by law.

We require the people and businesses we share information with to protect it and use it only for the purpose we gave it to them.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store or process information outside Australia. This includes Meta, our CRM provider and our website host, which may store data in the United States and other countries where their servers are located. Members of our agency's team who help manage our marketing and enquiries may also be located outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to make sure overseas recipients handle your information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

7. Marketing messages

If you enquire with us, we may contact you by phone, SMS or email about your project and, occasionally, about our services. Every marketing SMS or email will include a way to opt out (for example, reply STOP or click unsubscribe), or you can simply tell us. Opting out of marketing won't affect messages about a quote or job you've asked for.

8. How we store and protect it

We store personal information electronically in secure, password-protected systems with access limited to people who need it. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, change or disclosure.

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, including warranty, tax and legal requirements. When we no longer need it, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

9. Automated decisions

We don't use computer programs to make decisions about you that could significantly affect your rights or interests. Our advertising platforms use automated systems to decide who sees our ads, as described in section 4.

10. Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it if it's wrong, out of date or incomplete. Contact us using the details below. We'll respond within a reasonable time, usually 30 days. There's no charge to ask, and we'll explain our reasons if we can't give access or make a correction.

11. Questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first so we can try to fix it. Tell us what happened and how you'd like it resolved. We'll look into it and respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
